# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 28, 2026

This application ("the App") shows you current weather information and matching weather images based on your location, and can use a photo you choose or take to create a personalized weather background. We take your privacy seriously. Below you can read exactly what data we use, why, and how long we keep it.

## 1. What Data Do We Use?

* **Location Data (GPS):** to show you the right weather information and background images for your area, we use your location. Your most recently used location(s) are stored on your own device, so the App can show the right weather right away when it starts.
* **Photos you take or choose:** if you take a photo or pick one from your gallery to use as a personal weather background, that photo is sent to our server to be processed. If your photo contains location data, we use it to link the image to the right place.
* **A technical device identifier (for notifications):** to be able to notify you when your personal background is ready, we register a technical identifier for your device (a push notification token). This is not linked to your name or email address.

## 2. Why Do We Use This Data?

* **Location:** to show you relevant, location-specific weather information.
* **Photos:** to create a personalized weather background at your request, and to be able to show that same place faster and better in the future — including to other users who request a background of the same location.
* **Device identifier:** to let you know when your background is ready.

## 3. How Long Do We Keep This Data?

* **On your own device:** your recent locations stay stored locally until you delete them yourself or remove the App.
* **On our server:** photos you upload and the location data of those photos are kept indefinitely. This is intentional: it keeps a weather background, once created for a location, available quickly and reliably — including for other users.
* **Push notification token:** this refreshes itself automatically whenever you use the App, and is automatically deleted as soon as your device stops responding to it (for example after you uninstall the App) or after 12 months of inactivity.

## 4. Can a Photo Be Linked Back to You Personally?

No. We do not keep user accounts, and we never link a name, email address, or other personally identifying information to a photo or location. Our system is also built so that **the IP address of your internet connection is never recorded**: it is not logged and never stored alongside a photo or location. This means an uploaded photo can never be linked back to your IP address or internet connection after it is sent.

## 5. Sharing of Data

We never sell or rent your data to third parties. It is used solely to make the App work (showing weather and creating your personal background), not for advertising or profiling.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

* **Disable location access:** at any time through your Android device settings. The App will then no longer be able to show location-specific information.
* **Request deletion of a photo you submitted:** contact us at the email address below.
